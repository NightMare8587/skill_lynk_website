Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 11, 2026

Welcome to SkillLynk. We value your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application and website.

1. Introduction

SkillLynk ("we," "us," or "our") provides an AI-based interview practice platform. By using our services, you consent to the data practices described in this policy.

2. Information We Collect

We collect information that you provide directly to us:

  • Profile Information: Name, age, gender, experience, and resume details provided during onboarding.
  • Authentication Data: Phone numbers, OTP verification data, and Google account identifiers.
  • Communication Data: Audio and video data transmitted during mock interviews conducted via Agora RTC SDK. We do not store recordings of these calls unless explicitly stated for specific feedback purposes with your prior consent.
  • Wallet Transactions: Records of credit purchases, package selections, and usage history.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected data to:

  • Facilitate mock interviews between peers and experts.
  • Process wallet transactions and manage interview credits via secure payment gateways.
  • Provide feedback, ratings, and performance analytics.
  • Improve our AI algorithms and platform experience.
  • Send technical notices, security alerts, and support messages.

4. Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell your personal data. We may share information with:

  • Other Users: Limited profile information (name, experience, interests) is visible to potential interviewers for scheduling purposes.
  • Service Providers: Third-party vendors who provide services on our behalf, including Agora.io (real-time communication) and payment processors.
  • Legal Compliance: If required to do so by law or in response to valid requests by public authorities.

5. Data Security

We implement industry-standard administrative, technical, and physical security measures to protect your data. While we strive to use commercially acceptable means to protect your personal information, we cannot guarantee its absolute security.

6. Your Rights and Account Deletion

You have the right to access, update, or delete your information. You may delete your account at any time through the app settings, which will remove your personal data from our active databases, subject to legal retention requirements for financial transactions.

7. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at skill.lynkk@gmail.com.
