How it works

From practice to an interview

Practise

AI mock interviews, daily challenges and practice for your field. Everything you do is recorded on your Skill Passport.

Show your evidence

Your passport shows dated, verifiable cards: interview scores, challenges, strong topics. You choose which cards to show.

Apply to a bar you meet

Each program lists its requirements and tells you what is missing. Apply with one tap once you meet them all.

Get a reply

The company sends an interview invitation or a decline within the days it promised. If it does not, we tell you.

Straight talk

What fast-track is, and is not

It is a promise to reply

An interview or a decline, within the stated days. Each company's on-time record is published here once it has 5 applications.

It is not a job guarantee

Meeting the bar earns you a reply. The company still decides who to hire.

You decide what is shared

Nothing is shared until you apply. Applying shares your name, phone number, email and the evidence cards you are showing with that company only.
