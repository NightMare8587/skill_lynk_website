Four ways to practice

Real conditions, not flashcards

Every mode ends in real feedback, not a self-assessment. New accounts get 1,000 free credits, and peer interviews, practice and the Daily Challenge are free.

Peer mock interviews

Get matched live with another candidate, or schedule one ahead of time — 100% free, with AI-generated questions keeping the round on track. You take turns as interviewer and candidate.

AI voice interviews

Talk through a live mock interview with an AI interviewer that knows your field and level. Afterwards you get a full report: scores for accuracy, depth, clarity, structure and confidence, and a stronger sample answer for each question.

Judgment Practice

Realistic workplace situations from your field — an angry client, a missed deadline, a tricky negotiation. Every option is graded from best to harmful, with why.

Hiring Drives

Companies run real multi-round assessments on SkillLynk — a quiz, an AI interview and, for technical roles, a coding round. Free to enter, and strong candidates are shortlisted.
